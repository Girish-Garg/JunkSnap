# A confident "no" vetoes a report photo, recorded in combiner.version

Context: on prod an indoor room reached the map. YOLO 0, embeddings 0.77, the judge said not_waste (support 0.05), and the disagreement rule forced community_check (issue 103, PR 105).

Decision:
- Report photos only; cleanups are unchanged. A veto sets the decision to `rejected` whatever the combiner says, disagreement included.
- `judge_veto:not_waste`: the judge answers `not_waste` with claim support under 0.2. A weaker not_waste, or any other answer, falls through to the combiner.
- `detectors_veto:no_waste`: every non-neutral detector scores under 0.2, and the judge's support is under `community_check_min` (0.5) or the judge could not run. A judge at 0.5 to 0.85 leaves it to community_check.
- Evidence: `combiner.version` gets the suffix `+<reason> by <provider>/<model>@<version>`, one model per source, comma-separated, for example `meta-v1+judge_veto:not_waste by bedrock/apac.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0@v1`.
- The app reads `verification.judge.report_type`: `not_waste` gets the "doesn't look like a waste site" message; any other model rejection gets "Our checks couldn't confirm waste in this photo".

Consequences for other lanes: no schema change. The server's rejected path already keeps vetoed reports off sites and backers. The `verification_result` description's "strong disagreement always gives community_check" should be reworded in the next contracts PR.

Rejected alternatives: a veto for a matching type with low support (the confident not_waste covers the real case); a new `veto` field now (a schema change during the event).
