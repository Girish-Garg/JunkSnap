# Scan becomes an area scout: the models name each frame's type and place; frames of one spot make one site

Context: the lead asked for video to need no Type or "Where is it?" pick (issue 107), which authorised an additive contract change. Field bug the same day: a 6 s clip of nothing showed "7 reports made" and left 7 overlapping "1 of 10" sites from one person.

Decision:
- `scan_session` `report_type` and `context` take `auto` (not a separate `mode`): it maps one-to-one to the two chips, keeps both fields required, and the schema enforces the pairs (`auto` type needs `auto` context; `no_bin` never `auto`). The verify request takes `auto` the same way; ml always runs the judge for it.
- `report_upload`, `site` and `verification_result` stay unchanged. A scout frame row carries `waste_dump`/`other` plus table-only `auto_claim`, and takes the judge's type and context after verification; `not_waste` or `no_bin` rejects it. `no_bin` is never found from video.
- Frames of one scan within 20 m of a spot's first frame share table-only `scan_spot`, and frames of one spot and type merge into one site unless an existing neighbour site takes the frame; the spot site has a deterministic id (`sha256(scan_id:spot:type)`) and ignores embeddings. The merger reads it by id first and retries up to 11 times on a conflict.
- The worker drops near-duplicate frames: difference hash within 20 bits of a kept frame less than 10 m away.
- `GET /scans/{id}` gets `found`: distinct sites by type from passed frames, frames still checking, and each frame's result with its blurred copy. The app never counts frame reports as findings.

Consequences for other lanes: frontend shows Auto chips by default and the found line and frame strip in Activity. Typed frames still rely on the judge veto (PR #105) to reject a not-waste frame that the detectors disagree on. For a scout claim YOLO and the embedding head stay neutral (they score one claimed type), so Rekognition labels and the judge decide, and `nearby_verified_reports` counts the placeholder type; both are known gaps until the stacker is trained on scout rows.

Rejected alternatives: `mode: scout` (a third field that duplicates the chips); a new report type or a new `verification_result` purpose (changes enums that sites and letters read); serialising a scan's frames through one queue group (needs a FIFO queue).
