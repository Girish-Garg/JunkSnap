# JunkSnap frontend design

Playful on top of map-app patterns (decision 20261010-1502, which replaces the minimal look of 20261010-0201). The map stays quiet and the reports are the colour on it. The chrome has a personality: a cartoon camera logo, rounded headings, a sunny Report button and springy motion.

## Brand

- Mark: a chunky yellow camera with a thick ink outline, a crumpled paper ball for a lens, a coral shutter button and three flash pops. The file is `public/brand/junksnap-mark.svg`; the `Logo` component shows it with the two-tone wordmark (Junk in ink, Snap in teal).
- Icons are in `public/brand/`: `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and the lockups; a 432 px Android adaptive set is in `public/brand/android/`. `favicon.svg` is the mark on a teal tile (a charcoal tile in dark mode) and `favicon.ico` holds 16 and 32 px with the fine details dropped. `uv run scripts/make_icons.py` makes all of them, plus the Android launcher sources in `apps/android-tauri/` (then `npx tauri icon app-icon.json` there), from `junksnap-mark.svg`.

## Layout

- Phones (below 1024 px): the map fills everything above a 68 px bottom bar with five slots: Map, Areas, Report, Activity, Account. Report is a 60 px yellow sticker button (2 px ink outline, hard 3 px shadow, a 5 px surface ring) raised 24 px into the map. The bar paints above the map, so the button is never clipped, and the bar pads for `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- Top of the map: a 48 px search field with the 28 px logo mark (12 px padding and 12 px gap, one centre line), "Search Jodhpur" and the EN / हि toggle. Under it are the Filters pill with the Map / List toggle (phones) and a one-line status toast.
- List view (phones): List opens a sheet of the sites in view (or Jodhpur's when the view has none) as compact cards: photo, type, area, a status chip ("Waiting · 3 of 10", "Verified", "1 of 3 say cleared"), reports and distance. Sort by nearest (the default with a location), newest or most backed; a "Needs confirming" chip (shown when some sites are waiting) puts the waiting sites first, nearest first; Filters apply. A row flies the map to the site and opens its preview card, whose previous / next arrows (and a sideways swipe) step through the list; back returns to the list, and a second tap on that row opens the site.
- Sheets are bottom sheets with three heights (peek 156 px, half, and full minus 80 px and the status bar inset). They follow the finger: drag the handle bar, or drag down from content scrolled to the top; release snaps to the nearest height, a throw moves one height, and a drag below the peek height or a throw down from peek closes the sheet (`SheetGesture`). Content scrolls at every height. On phones, focusing a text field opens the sheet to full and hides the bottom bar while the keyboard is up. The viewport keeps the browser's default for the keyboard (no `interactive-widget`). In Chrome the keyboard overlays the page and only the visual viewport shrinks, so the map, search field and pins do not jump when someone searches. The Android app's WebView resizes the whole window. `useTypingLayer` follows `visualViewport`, which works for both. Items in a sheet that own a drag (camera, canvas, the Snap target map, the photo stage) carry `data-own-drag`, and the sheet ignores pointer events from portaled children. On tablets (640 to 1023 px) they keep a 600 px width.
- Desktop (1024 px and up): a 420 px side panel on the left holds the logo, tab pills (Map, Areas, Activity, Account), search and Filters. Under those it shows the open sheet, or "In view", the list of reports on the map. The map is on the right. Scan and Report sit at its top right and open the "use your phone" dialog with a QR code of the app's address (the code loads lazily, so phones never download it). The status toast floats at the top of the map.
- Start: a splash of at most 1.2 s (the camera pops in, the paper ball rolls into the lens, the flash pops, then the mark flies into the search field logo). Any tap or key skips it; under reduced motion the mark shows still. On the first visit to the map the "How JunkSnap works" cards open once (`/welcome`, 4 swipeable cards: why, how it works, who looks after a site, scores and sign-in). Account reopens them.
- Back (decision 20261011-0030, proposed): each open sheet is one history level. Every sheet, card or dialog that is not a route (sign-in, Filters, the pin preview card or stack list, the area card, the phone dialog) pushes a history entry (`useHistoryLayer`). The pin preview card, the stack list and the area card share one entry (`usePickLayer`), so one replacing another (a pin tapped with an area's card up) reuses it, and a hidden card is unmounted, never left to catch Escape. Route sheets open with a depth and the trail of paths underneath in router state, and close with history back (`useSheetNavigation`, never a bare `navigate`); opening one first unwinds the map's own layers, so nothing stays underneath. A change that keeps the entry (a ranking filter in the address) carries that state along. Tabs replace each other. A tab and "All areas" go `up(path)`: back to that screen when it is underneath (a tab tapped inside its own section returns to its first screen), else in place of the current sheet. Close, the Android and browser back buttons, Escape, a drag down and, on phones, a tap on the map each close exactly the topmost sheet, and the app is left only from the map. A text field with the keyboard up is one more level: the first back or Escape lets go of the field, not the sheet. The pin preview card has a close button and closes with back, Escape and a swipe down too.

## Tokens (`src/ui/tokens.css`)

- Type: Baloo 2 for headings and numbers, Hanken Grotesk for text, Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi text (`:lang(hi)`). The scale is caption 12/16, small 13/18, body 15/22, lead 17/24, title 26/30 and headline 32/36. Inputs are 16 px.
- Spacing: a 4 px scale (`--s-1` 4 to `--s-12` 48). Screen edges are 16 px on phones and 24 px on desktop. Use only the scale, through the spacing roles below.

- Radii: 8, 12, 16, 22, 30 and pill. Sheets use 30, cards 16 to 22, inputs 12; buttons are pills.
- Colour: page `#fbf6ee`, surface `#fffdf8`, tile `#f4ecdf`, text `#221c15` and `#5f5446`, accent teal `#0b7a73` (white text 5.2:1), sun `#ffc23d` for Report and the logo, and coral `#ff7a59` only inside illustrations. Dark mode keeps the roles with warm charcoal neutrals and a lighter teal. There are no party colours: no saffron, and no saffron-white-green or other flag combinations.
- Map areas: every seat on the map has a neighbour tint, so one area reads apart from the next. `--area-tint-1` to `--area-tint-8` are greyed ochre `#a38a4e`, olive `#7a8f22`, teal `#1f8f86`, slate `#4a6fa8`, mauve `#9c4f93`, clay `#b0603a`, stone `#706b5f` and rose `#c4567a` (lighter versions in dark mode). A graph colouring (`AreaColouring`, Welsh-Powell, least-used free slot) gives each area a slot no neighbour has, even neighbours that meet only at a corner. A test checks this on the shipped `areas.geojson`. Fills are drawn at 12 to 15%, and the chips in lists, the card and the legend use the same 15% wash. Why this palette is neutral: every party in India uses a strong saffron, green, blue or red, often in flag combinations. These tints are low-chroma and spread round the colour wheel, so no single hue dominates the map. At 15% over the sand basemap they read as pale washes, never as a party's colour, and none is a flag colour (saffron `#ff9933`, India green `#138808`, navy). A test keeps every tint whose hue falls near saffron, green, blue or red at HSL saturation 0.55 or less. The legend says "colours only tell neighbours apart". A seat with a shown score (10+ verified reports) takes its score colour instead, at 30% at most so it never reads as an alarm, and its marker shows the score with its count. Edges are `--text-secondary`. The area in focus (tapped, else open, else where the person stands) gets a thick accent outline and a marker that is always shown. A tap opens the area card with the name, counts, status and Open area. Areas tab rows and the card carry the same tint as a chip, but not for a scored area, whose map colour is its score colour.
- Report types: dump `#c5221f`, no bin `#e37400`, water `#1967d2`, construction `#5d4037`, overflowing or broken bin `#7b3fa6`. Chips and lists use hand-drawn type icons (`TypeIcon`: heap, crossed bin, drop, cone, open bin spilling over) filled with the type colour on a soft tint tile; map pins keep the round glyph discs. Colour is never the only cue.
- Icons: the key icons are hand-drawn components in `src/ui/icons/` (one file each, 24 grid, 2 px rounded outline): map with a pin, neighbourhood with a flag, the logo camera, clipboard with a tick, a friendly face, sliders, target, share and printer. At rest they are outlines; the current tab or a pressed pill fills the two tones (sun and teal tint) and hops. The small utility icons (close, search, chevrons) stay plain paths in `Icon`.
- Appearance: Account → Settings offers System (the default), Light and Dark (`ThemePreference`, localStorage key `junksnap.theme`). An inline script in `index.html` sets `data-theme` before first paint. The basemap switches to its dark palette in place (`BasemapTheme.paint`); runways, aprons and rail dashes take the quiet road tones so no white bar crosses the dark map, and labels never print an OSM placeholder name such as "Nan" (`PlaceholderNames`).
- Language: Account → Settings also offers English and हिन्दी (the same choice as the EN / हि toggle). In list mode, where the search field is hidden, the toggle sits beside Filters.
- Status chips (`SiteStatusChip`): a dot plus the words, on a tint, one per site on the pin card, the site sheet and the lists: "Waiting · 1 of 10" in warm neutral, "Verified" in teal, "Cleared" in green, "Reopened" in amber, and a closed site in grey. A normal state is never red. On the pin card and the site sheet the chip and the "n of 10" count carry a small info mark: a tap opens a short explainer (`StatusExplainer`, one history level) of what the state means and what to do next.
- Pin card: a compact map-app place card above the bottom bar: a handle, an 80 px rounded photo, the type icon and a one-line title (ellipsis), the status chip, "1 report · 3.5 km", then a full-width View site with round Add a photo and Share buttons (48 px). A tap anywhere on it opens the site; a swipe down closes it. Every card over the map (`useMapCard`) stands on the map foot and publishes its measured height as `--card-height` on the map page, and the map buttons sit above it.
- Site sheet: the title, status chip, distance and the ward, MLA seat and MP seat line come first, then the actions in one row, so the half-open sheet shows them above the Report button; then the backers bar (with "Ask neighbours to confirm", the share link with a short message, while the site waits for its 10 people), the clearing progress and expiry, the photo carousel with its count ("3 photos") and Add a photo, and one outlined card per section (who reported it, bins nearby, notes, location, brands, history).
- Elevation: soft warm shadows for floating things, and a hard ink "sticker" shadow for the chunky ones (Report, the primary site action, the success tick).

## Spacing rules

One source per space: a flex or grid `gap` spaces its children, and a child adds no margin of its own on top of it (a doubled margin plus gap is the most common slip). Each rule has a token in `tokens.css`.

1. Card padding, `--card-pad` (16 px): every outlined or tinted card (site sections, backers, clearing, notes, votes, Activity and feedback cards, the letter, the account card, the pin and area cards) pads 16 px on every side. A card with a handle keeps 8 px above it. One-line notes and banners pad `--s-3` by `--card-pad`.
2. Gap between cards, `--card-gap` (12 px): stacked cards in a list or a sheet sit 12 px apart.
3. Section spacing, `--section-gap` (24 px): the blocks of a sheet (header, actions, progress, photos, the first card of a stack, a form, the letter, Settings and its divider) are 24 px apart. A note that belongs to the block above it (the sample note under the letter banner) sits `--inline-gap` under it instead.
4. Inline gaps, `--inline-gap` (8 px): chips, an icon and its label, a row of buttons. A text button's label sits on the content edge like the text around it; its tap area and hover pill reach `--text-bleed` (12 px) past the label, so a row that holds text buttons leaves `--s-6` between them.
5. The sheet edge, `--sheet-edge` (`--edge`): content sits this far from the sheet's sides, and a row that scrolls past them (the photo strip) bleeds to the screen and pads and snaps back to it, so its first item never touches the screen edge.

Over the map, floating things stand on the map foot, `--map-foot` (Report's overhang plus 8 px): the map buttons, the Legend chip (clear of MapLibre's credit button below it) and the pin and area cards. With a card open, the map buttons sit `--inline-gap` above its measured `--card-height`, published before paint.

## Motion

- Springs come from CSS `linear()` (`--spring`, `--spring-bouncy`); there is no animation library. Sheets spring up and slide away (`useExitAnimation`). Content rises a beat after its sheet, lists stagger, and the selected tab pill pops.
- Buttons press down into their shadow and squash. Report wiggles every 7 s.
- Map: pins drop in with a bounce when sites first arrive (`PinDrop`); the selected pin gets a landing splash and then a breathing ring (`SelectedPulse`), and the user dot pings.
- Delight: confetti and a self-drawing tick when a report or video is saved, confetti when a vote counts, and a hopping logo while a letter drafts. Skeletons sweep.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` cuts every animation to 1 ms and hides the confetti.

## States

- Empty states use small cartoons in the logo's style (`Illustration`): a happy bin (nothing here), a sleepy unplugged cloud (could not load) and the camera with its lens shut (camera blocked).
- Few reports (`ThinView`): when a settled view holds 2 sites or fewer, phones show a small card on the map foot with the real count of sites on the map ("No sites on the map here right now", "Only 1 site on the map here right now"), the happy bin, Report the first one (sign-in as usual), See areas and, only when the app already holds sites outside the view and the wider view still loads whole, Zoom out (fitted between the search stack and the card). On a short phone (720 px tall or less) it keeps only the count and the actions. It keeps its last answer through a pan or a load, springs in only the first time, hides under any card, sheet or the list, and Close hides it for the browser session; while it is up, the toast leaves out "no reports here" (desktop and a dismissed card keep the toast). The list shows the same note under one or two of the view's own rows when no filter is on.
- The toast gives one line for each of: offline, load failed, location blocked, filters hiding everything, "zoom in", sites just off screen (with a "Show the nearest report" button), and a truly empty view. It never says "No reports here yet" while the search around the view is still running. Offline is a wait, so its line is amber like the sheets' offline line; only something that failed is red.
- No bin here sends at once and then shows the same sent screen as a photo: the tick, what the report does, Report another and See all in Activity. The report is in Activity straight away, as the same row the server's list later updates.
- Account: Sign in is the main action when signed out; Sign out is a quiet text button at the foot, after Settings.
- Opening view: always Jodhpur, the pilot city (`PilotCity`), for judges, first visits and people with no location. If the first view loads empty and nothing else has moved the map (the person, a search or a deep link), it fits Jodhpur's sites once. A location fix (read on open only when location is already allowed) shows the dot but never moves the map; "Go to my location" does.
- Actions that are off (decision 20261011-0303): a reason the person must act on (own site, too far, letters waiting for 10 people, offline, rate limited) shows the action muted with `aria-disabled` and a dashed tile, and a tap says why in the app toast at the top; a short wait (sending, camera starting, first GPS fix) is plainly disabled; a sign-in reason looks normal and opens sign-in with the reason. Every sheet shows a line while offline, and a crashed sheet shows "Something went wrong" with Try again.
- Mock mode: Filters carries a small "Sample data" note, and code fields show the sample code.

## Rules kept from 0201

- 48 px touch targets, a 2 px focus ring, and AA contrast in both themes.
- No gradients used as decoration, no glass and no purple. Show no names of individuals anywhere.
