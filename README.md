# JunkSnap

**Snap the junk. Prove it. Get it cleared.**

Dumps are not only the authorities' failure. People dump on empty plots, often because there is nowhere legal to put waste. JunkSnap measures all three causes.

Anyone in India snaps waste with the in-app camera. JunkSnap blurs faces and number plates, verifies the report with a stacked ensemble of models, merges it into a site on the map, finds its ward, assembly seat and Lok Sabha seat, and drafts a letter to the body that must act, with every quoted rule checked word for word against the official text. Each area gets three scores: how fast the city clears dumps, how much residents dump, and whether people have anywhere legal to put waste.

Pilot city: Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

Built for Environmental Hacks (Bharat Builds Tour by WeMakeDevs and AWS), 8 to 11 October 2026, Waste and Energy track. Team: Hemang, Girish, Harshit.

## Limits

- Satellite and street photos show that waste exists, not who put it there. JunkSnap never names a person.
- Letters are drafts, not legal advice. The resident files them; nothing is sent automatically.
